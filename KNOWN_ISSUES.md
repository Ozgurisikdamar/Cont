# Known issues

Every number below comes from a file in `reports/`: the **locked holdout**
(`reports/locked/results.json`, evaluated once after the freeze, run 2 — see
decisions.md D-36), the development evaluation (`reports/evaluation_dev.json`)
or an experiment file (`reports/experiments/*.json`). Severity: **high** =
visible to most users, **medium** = visible in some situations, **low** =
cosmetic or operational.

| # | issue | severity | cause | impact (measured) | attempted | recommended |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Off-topic text still often gets a topic** | high | the model has seen only the 8 topics; the gate is a distance rule | locked: 78.1% of assistant chat (CLINC150) and 48.5% of off-topic Stack Exchange questions are flagged; dev: only 24% of out-of-taxonomy Wikipedia *passages* | seven detectors compared (D-34); Mahalanobis chosen; two detectors trained on chat catch 96–97% of chat but fail on non-chat off-topic text | human-labelled off-topic chat from the target users; a detector trained on it, evaluated on text that is not assistant commands |
| 2 | **Science on real questions** | high | Science now means the scientific enterprise (D-32); most history-of-science questions are about the history of one field | locked: Science F1 0.656 on Wikipedia but 0.241 on questions; hsm accuracy 0.19; 42% of hsm questions → Physics | label redefinition + audit-driven exclusions (D-32) | treat hsm as a mixed site (reported separately already); allow a second general topic ("Physics + history") |
| 3 | **Multi-label is a suggestion, not a prediction** | medium | only 6% of passages carry two subtopics; every sigmoid head's optimum predicts one label | dev: a second gold subtopic is found for 5.7% of multi-label passages (one-vs-rest sigmoid: 11.8%, at −1.1 macro-F1) | three heads compared (D-33) | more multi-label data; keep the wording "primary subtopic + sibling suggestions" |
| 4 | **Weak subtopics** | medium | overlapping definitions, small locked support (14–17 per label) | locked Wikipedia F1: classical mechanics 0.33, evolution 0.42, cell biology 0.43, history of science 0.44, relativity 0.46; locked questions: history of science 0.17 | label audit and fixes (D-32) | more distinctive seeds; "general only" answers when no subtopic is confident |
